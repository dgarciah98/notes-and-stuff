# GITADORA FUZZ-UP — 個人差リスト · DrumMania (全レベル)

> Songs where difficulty **varies significantly depending on the player's individual strengths and weaknesses**. DrumMania only. Sorted by level (ascending). All difficulty brackets included.

---

- **Lv6.50 I LOVE COSMOS** (DM/EXT)
    
    - HH+BP→SN cross-style-friendly patterns dominate. Even players at the appropriate level who can only play open stance may end up with B–C rank, and above-level players may face a mid-song dropout. The 16th-note triple tom run repeating in the chorus is busy — connection difficulty is on par with "I LOVE SUNFLOWER" EXT.
- **Lv6.50 アナーキーインザ夕景** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The MAS onslaught patterns are gone, but the off-rhythm SN section remains, and that part can still affect accuracy. Notably, the hand note count is almost the same as MAS, and without the LP in the dance-beat section, it can actually be harder to maintain accuracy in some cases.
- **Lv6.60 Juicy** (DM/EXT)
    
    - ⚠️ Warning! HH+BP→SN cross-style-friendly patterns dominate. Even players at the appropriate level who can only play open stance may end up with B–C rank, and above-level players may face a mid-song dropout. →旧曲
- **Lv6.70 ルミナスデイズ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Composed entirely of 8th notes with no 16th-note patterns at all. Many sections have toms interspersed mid-strumming, and there are also many patterns that push you to the right such as FT+CY. Individual variation depends on familiarity with open style and left hand mobility.
- **Lv7.45 She saw a miracle** (DM/MAS)
    
    - The ska-style offbeat patterns are the main content and easy to groove to, but accuracy varies depending on how well you handle the 16th-note SN roll in the B section and the tom combinations including 24th-note runs.
- **Lv7.80 What's up?** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Simplified strumming, fills, and LP quarter-note-focused technique chart. Whether it's a skill earner depends on your skill bracket.
- **Lv8.30 Behind the song of stars** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Simplified strumming, fills, and LP quarter-note-focused technique chart. Whether it's a skill earner depends on your skill bracket.
- **Lv8.30 AVEL** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Compared to MAS, the difficulty gap is relatively small (~1.00), but the tricky difficult sections are greatly simplified, making it a chart that purely tests technique and precision. Compared to the Lv8.10 逆詐称-rated EXT in this version, the required technique is somewhat higher — whether it's a skill earner depends on your skill bracket.
- **Lv8.30 Secret Raid** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Compared to MAS, the difficulty gap is relatively small (~1.00), but the tricky difficult sections are greatly simplified, making it a chart that purely tests technique and precision. Compared to the Lv8.10 逆詐称-rated EXT in this version, the required technique is somewhat higher — whether it's a skill earner depends on your skill bracket.
- **Lv8.40 I LOVE uUu ～Holy Graduation～** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Specialized in BPM 190 2-beat strumming and 16th-note BP double runs. Accuracy varies greatly depending on how well you handle these patterns, but there are no notable difficult sections or combo-killers, so if they're a strength, high skill scores are possible even above level.
- **Lv8.40 Onion Ninja** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Features variable-speed runs that switch between 16th and 24th notes, and many tom-axis wide movements, making individual variation very high. BPM is slow and density is low, but the low note count makes it surprisingly hard to maintain high precision.
- **Lv8.40 我甘党** (DM/MAS)
    
    - BPM 210 16th-note double bass appears in the early section at this difficulty. Everything after is level-appropriate, so accuracy and rank are determined by that section.
- **Lv8.50 TOGETOGE feat.パンダの流儀** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Overall the chart is relaxed for the difficulty, but the two backbeat double-bass sections (16th-note offbeat BP with LP on the beat — similar pattern to "Sonne") cause individual variation. It's an uncommon pattern and without preparation can throw off your form. Also watch out for the opening FT2-LC+SN.
- **Lv8.50 I LOVE SUNFLOWER** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Specialized in BPM 190 2-beat strumming and 16th-note BP double runs. Accuracy varies greatly depending on how well you handle these patterns, but there are no notable difficult sections or combo-killers, so if they're a strength, high skill scores are possible even above level.
- **Lv8.70 累乗のカルマ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Many unconventional patterns where HH strumming is partially replaced by LP, with 16th-note HH runs also mixed in, making rhythm difficult to track and precision hard to maintain. Fills also have many overtaking and wide-movement sections, and without familiarity, achieving high accuracy is difficult. Late-chart fills also require one-hand processing, raising connection difficulty too. Slightly on the 詐称 side.
- **Lv8.80 スイーツはとまらない♪** (DM/MAS)
    
    - The orthodox sections from EXT now have 16th-note offbeat SN added. At BPM 204 and hitting continuously throughout, appropriate stamina and concentration are also required. The pattern is unique and not seen in other songs — individual variation is very high. The 16th-note tom fills in between are also surprisingly easy to drop, making this a chart that never lets you relax.
- **Lv8.80 ミックスナッツ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Mainly repeating 8th-note offbeat LP while doing 16th-note CY triple runs. Individual variation splits depending on whether CY is processed with one or two hands, but from the chorus onward the patterns become more relaxed — try to build accuracy there. For connection, watch the early SN triple-run sections. Note: on the コナステ side this is rated Lv7.80, a notably inflated rating.
- **Lv9.00 真っ白な闇がすべてを塗り替えても** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Double bass and hard-to-read tom runs appear from the very beginning, and CY-mixed tom runs also make it hard to stabilize form. It's a strengthened version of the same artist's "終わらぬ涙の海で" MAS — with some stamina and double-bass tolerance as a baseline, high accuracy requires hand technique to match.
- **Lv9.05 Duality** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Many wide-movement fills and tom runs difficult to process with singles, with BPM 190 double bass appearing throughout. Naturally, if you can't keep up with that speed the majority of phrases will be dropped, but overall the patterns for Lv9.05 are relaxed and the note count is low at 990. Accuracy varies heavily with high-speed double-bass tolerance — overall slightly on the 逆詐称 side.
- **Lv9.20 朧月覆う雲をも裂きぬ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - A pure stamina chart with 1440 notes. Strumming frequency is low, but the long double-bass runs at BPM 192 and the pre-chorus 12th-note forced patterns have large individual variation. Outside the final 24th-note tom run (BPM 288 16th-note equivalent), there are almost no cutpoints — if you have the technique and stamina to connect up to there, high accuracy from that point is an individual matter.
- **Lv9.40 Black Apple** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Frequent HH-SN-axis wide movements throughout the chart are its defining feature, with 24th-note runs mixed in immediately after, making phrase drops easy. The patterns vary subtly based on the above framework, requiring enough chart-reading ability to keep up. Mid-chart has LT/FT-driven tom runs, and late-chart has complex HH runs with toms mixed in — both are difficult to process with singles. Technique-focused chart, but slightly on the 詐称 side.
- **Lv9.55 ちくわパフェだよ☆ＣＫＰ (Yvya Remix)** (DM/MAS)
    
    - A chart that demands high stamina and endurance with BPM 205 2-beat strumming and 16th-note double bass. If you can't keep up with the speed, both rank and accuracy will suffer badly, but outside the mid-chart 5-beat section the patterns are honest — players with high BPM and double-bass tolerance will find it relatively manageable. For connection, the LC 4-run mixed into the fills and the pre-chorus 24th-note linear phrase are the main obstacles.
- **Lv9.60 Hard luck, Tough luck** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Strong off-beat rhythm difficulty typical of OSTER songs, with complex 16th-note RC combined with inconsistent LP strumming. High technique required — above-level players will often be overwhelmed. However, there are no extremely high-density sections outside the CY 24th-note run (BPM 231 16th-note equivalent), and everything else is a series of intricate fill patterns. Note: the CY run's endpoint crosses into the next phrase, so depending on where the combo breaks, the next phrase may be pulled in as well.
