# GITADORA — 個人差リスト · DrumMania (全レベル)

> Songs where difficulty **varies significantly depending on the player's individual strengths and weaknesses**. DrumMania only. Sorted by level (ascending). All difficulty brackets included. Source: General GITADORA series wiki (all versions).

---

- **Lv1.70 天ノ弱** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Perceived difficulty varies greatly depending on whether you can hit CY or HH alone in quarter notes. The song has high name recognition and is frequently selected by casual players, but without understanding strumming and rhythm to some degree, FAILED is likely.
- **Lv2.00 BRE∀K DOWN! (GF&DM STYLE)** (DM/BSC)
    
    - A chart that makes you hit various pads including simultaneous-hit sections. Individual variation appears depending on whether you know the pad locations.
- **Lv2.25 猫侍の逆襲** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Most of the chart is quarter-note hits or alternating with SN, and outside a few dotted-8th sections before the chorus and near the end, there are no standout difficult sections. However, the previous version's difficulty drop was large (-0.50: Lv2.75→2.25), making it slightly 詐称-leaning.
- **Lv2.55 LOVE♥SHINE** (DM/BSC)
    
    - **Lv2.80 Let's Dance** (DM/BSC)
    - Both have many BP quarter-note stomps and lean toward stamina charts. LOVE♥SHINE was promoted from the previous version's Lv2.30, but still leans toward 詐称.
- **Lv3.00 有頂天ビバーチェ (LONG)** (DM/BSC)
    
    - From mid-chart, 8th-note strumming proportion increases and LC 5-runs + 8th-note offbeat SN mixed HH/LC strumming appear. Perceived difficulty varies depending on familiarity with pad placement.
- **Lv3.10 Spring Pain** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Total note count of 343 is on the higher end for this difficulty. No particular difficult sections, but the high note density causes individual variation.
- **Lv3.60 LIKE A VAMPIRE** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Overall biased toward the left side (most notes assigned to LC, HH, SN), which some players may find uncomfortable to play.
- **Lv3.60 Slip Into My Royal Blood** (DM/BSC)
    
    - The long SN 8th-note run in the Intro (also involving LC and RC) causes accuracy variation. Not fast, so recovery after falling out of sync is not difficult.
- **Lv4.00 IMITATION TOUCH** (DM/BSC)
    
    - 8th-note consecutive BP appears near the end. An uncommon pattern at this level — first-sight players and those who struggle with it should be cautious.
- **Lv4.05 Fairy Tales** (DM/BSC)
    
    - A slightly fast 8th-note backbeat comes in the second half, plus 16th-note runs mixed in at the very end. Slightly advanced technique for this level.
- **Lv4.05 triangulum** (DM/BSC)
    
    - BPM 161 quarter-note alternating stomping appears in both the first and second halves, and players unfamiliar with LP are likely to be caught off guard. However, since no other pad notes are involved in those sections, focusing on both pedals is recommended.
- **Lv4.15 卒業** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Compared to other songs at the same level, this is a stamina chart with more BP quarter-note strumming and a faster tempo. Practicing BP stomping that doesn't tire you out is valuable here.
- **Lv4.40 黒髪乱れし修羅となりて** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Consistently alternating HH-SN chart. With open stance, the downbeat falls on the left hand making rhythm harder to track. The late section is milder than ADV, but BP mixing makes it slightly 詐称-leaning.
- **Lv4.50 AXIS** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Perceived difficulty varies depending on whether you can handle the odd-time section mid-chart and other rhythm-difficult patterns.
- **Lv4.60 Aion** (DM/BSC)
    
    - The Special first half (late chorus) is an LC+BD→SD→HH→SD repeating pattern biased to the left, meaning both cross and open style players may air-hit if they don't have a feel for pad placement.
- **Lv4.65 ナキムシの凱旋** (DM/BSC)
    
    - BPM 220 high-speed chart. Dotted rhythm and high-density sections that push you left and right make it a busy chart for appropriate-level players.
- **Lv4.70 Predator's Crypto Pt.2** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Odd time signatures continue from the very start, with hard-to-read simultaneous hit barrages. Everything after is also rhythm-difficult making PERFECT judgment hard to obtain.
- **Lv4.90 Destiny Lovers** (DM/BSC)
    
    - How well you handle the LC interspersed in the HH 8th-note runs is tested. The Special second half pushes you left and right, but from Phrase C onward it's relatively mild.
- **Lv4.90 JET WORLD** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Chart density is level-appropriate. More 16th-note runs than typical BSC making it easy to miss on runs at appropriate level causing individual variation. Variety of patterns including SN4-CY base form, HH-SN3-CY variations, HT2-LT-CY tom fills, and offbeat-entry SN triple runs makes it ideal for practice.
- **Lv5.00 Saiph** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Total note count is 700 and leans toward volume, but 8th-note pattern dominant with no 16th notes. Many busy patterns including tom combinations and BP→HH+SN repeats, causing individual variation based on chart recognition ability and stamina.
- **Lv5.00 遊戯大熊猫** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Intro and second half have sections repeating HT 16th-note doubles 4 times. Without understanding the tempo and rhythm, OK and MISS judgments appear easily.
- **Lv5.30 Last song** (DM/BSC)
    
    - No pedal notes appear at all. In exchange, hands are tricky with higher-than-usual tom frequency, and the variable-speed CY strumming mixed with SN and toms mid-chart causes breakdowns.
- **Lv5.40 正論** (DM/BSC)
    
    - BPM 284 high-speed tempo, but the A-section is mainly SN quarter-note backbeat. B-section has many tom runs (SN4-HT2-LT2-CY) requiring pad-to-pad movement. The chorus — unlike ADV and above — has HH→HH+SN quarter-note strumming (equivalent to BPM 142 8th-note strumming), making it ideal for learning strumming basics.
- **Lv5.50 MAD BLAST** (DM/BSC)
    
    - **Lv5.95 恐怖の右脳改革** (DM/BSC)
    - Both have tricky wide-movement sections, but outside those the charts are relatively mild, making perceived difficulty vary based on wide-movement tolerance. "MAD BLAST" has no difficult sections or notable density outside the left-right tom/SN wide sections. "恐怖の右脳改革" is confusing on first sight combined with odd time signatures. However, this level range has no run requirements, so players comfortable with wide movements will feel it's mid-Lv5 range.
- **Lv5.55 SIX DIMENSION** (DM/BSC)
    
    - **Lv6.25 ゲームより愛を込めて** (DM/BSC)
    - Both have many HH+BP→SN alternating hits. Cross style syncs right hand + right foot easily and is advantageous, but open hand makes left hand + right foot confusing, and unfamiliar players may chain-miss and dropout immediately. Both have 16th-note runs, slightly raising connection difficulty.
- **Lv5.65 KAISER PHOENIX** (DM/BSC)
    
    - A comprehensive technique chart with alternating hits, backbeat strumming, both cymbals, etc. while stomping quarter-note BP.
- **Lv5.75 EXCELSIOR DIVE** (DM/BSC)
    
    - BP is placed in a tricky dotted-16th rhythm. Players who struggle with BP may break rhythm and dropout.
- **Lv6.00 Dynamis** (DM/BSC)
    
    - While there are gaps, HH 16th-note runs appear — unusual for BSC. Also watch the wide-movement odd-time section.
- **Lv6.20 MODEL DD3** (DM/BSC)
    
    - A chart thinned out further from V-series BSC with extreme density variation. Note count alone suggests Lv5 range. However, the mild sections have odd time (15/16) making PERFECT rate hard, and locally 16th-note tom runs appear, making accuracy unexpectedly difficult.
- **Lv6.35 MODEL DD4** (DM/BSC)
    
    - The first half has only difficult HH 8th-note strumming sections, but from the second half, irregular SN and tom combinations appear with 16th-note tom runs mixed throughout. Among old boss BSC charts that are further thinned from V-series, this one doesn't differ much from the V series.
- **Lv6.50 Rock to Infinity** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Despite Lv6.50, no LP and few 8th/16th-note runs. However, sections hitting each tom one note at a time appear throughout, plus short FT strumming, HH-SN and LT-FT 16th-note alternating hits, requiring comprehensive skill outside strumming. Easy to clear if you know pad placement even if strumming is a weakness.
- **Lv6.60 over there** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Understanding the 3-beat based rhythm (6/8 time, partially 5/8) is important for clearing. Individual variation from CY-SN 16th-note alternating and even-count HH 16-beat mixed HH. Without understanding, confusion from the above time signatures compounds easily. Connection difficulty depends on whether main phrases are achievable, with tricky flams and single-processing-difficult tom runs (SN2-HT-LT-FT2) as the main obstacles.
- **Lv6.90 MODEL DD6** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Many 8th-note strumming, 16th-note unit fine strumming, and tom-linked fills and strumming appear. 8th-note consecutive BP appears in the second half, so without sufficient skill to handle the first half, you'll run out of steam.
- **Lv7.05 DAY DREAM** (DM/BSC)
    
    - Further thinned from ADV, but originally rhythm-difficult (17/16 time) making precision hard, and toms and CY doubles become involved toward the end. The final tom roll will produce OK or MISS if you can't recognize the note count. Sight-reading recommended.
- **Lv3.40 セツナトリップ** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Whether you can stay stable in the 8th-note interval alternating hits (HH→SN→HH→SN…) is the key, but the surrounding phrases make gauge recovery and rhythm correction easy.
- **Lv3.80 天ノ弱** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Compared to the same level, chart density is higher and BPM is fast at 200, but if you can handle HH and CY quarter-note strumming, clear difficulty is level-appropriate.
- **Lv3.80 狂鬼村正** (DM/ADV)
    
    - BPM 200 quarter-note strumming is the main content, with BP either simultaneous with bar-head or in half-note patterns except for the 8th-note doubles in the Intro. However, it's continuously hitting throughout for this difficulty, and without some volume tolerance, precision will break down in the second half.
- **Lv3.90 カタルシスの月** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Simple quarter-note strumming continues throughout with low density in the mid section. Players familiar with the song can clear with mid-Lv3 skill. Note: this chart has LP notes rare for late Lv3, but no other notes are combined with them making it ideal for practice.
- **Lv4.20 Sing A Well** (DM/ADV)
    
    - All 5 phrases contain SN 16th-note runs. Without the skill to reliably connect these, it's possible to not capture even a single phrase.
- **Lv4.40 LOVE♥SHINE** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Same as BSC — many BP quarter-note stomps and slightly stamina-chart leaning. Some backbeat HH alternating appears mid-chart, and individual variation from rhythm-keeping ability.
- **Lv4.50 †渚の小悪魔ラヴリィ～レイディオ† (GITADORA ver.)** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Occasional long SN 8th-note runs at BPM 190, with tom fill-ins at the same speed. These can be processed with singles without problem, but from the chorus onward you're continuously stomping BP quarter notes giving it a slight stamina-chart tendency.
- **Lv4.75 DRAGON KILLER** (DM/ADV)
    
    - 12th-note runs exist, but most of the song is bass-free quarter-note strumming making clear easy. However, isolated LP mid-chart means carelessness can lead to left-foot misses.
- **Lv4.75 ヴィーナスとジーザス** (DM/ADV)
    
    - **Lv4.85 Swimming In Love** (DM/ADV)
    - **Lv4.90 チョコレートスマイル** (DM/ADV)
    - All are mostly quarter-note strumming and 8th-note backbeat bass. Perceived difficulty varies based on whether basic hand-foot coordination is achievable. "Swimming In Love" has slightly more left-right wide movements due to pattern changes from V series. "チョコレートスマイル" — watch the mid-chart CY+tom combination and SN 16th-note triple run.
- **Lv4.95 ビター・エスケープ** (DM/ADV)
    
    - **Lv5.90 ホーンテッド★メイドランチ** (DM/ADV)
    - **Lv6.20 Clove** (DM/ADV)
    - All have very many backbeat bass for their level, potentially draining a large amount of gauge at appropriate level. "Clove" has consistent BPM 210 8th-note backbeat bass and requires the highest technique among this category.
- **Lv4.95 ちくわパフェだよ☆CKP** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Density is somewhat low for the level. Many pattern-heavy sections like CY+tom simultaneous hits and odd-time SN 8th→LC, causing large individual variation at appropriate level.
- **Lv5.00 群青と流星** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Quarter-note strumming and backbeat bass dominant chart — leans toward 逆詐称 for clear. However, HH 16th-note offbeat mixes in the B-section and interlude, and accuracy varies based on whether those can be handled.
- **Lv5.15 IX -GITADOROCK ver.-** (DM/ADV)
    
    - **Lv5.25 TOXIC VIBRATION** (DM/ADV)
    - **Lv5.30 DEATH BRINGER** (DM/ADV)
    - All three — perceived difficulty varies based on whether high-BPM quarter-note strumming and SN-axis alternating hits are achievable. Charts are simply structured and appropriate-level players can aim for full combo. "TOXIC VIBRATION" — watch the slight rhythm difficulty at LC at the B-section end and SN near the chorus end.
- **Lv5.25 風／雷** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Isolated LP appears frequently from the chorus onward. Most are placed after HH notes, so being conscious of that helps.
- **Lv5.35 Triple Journey -OJ- EDITION-** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Sections with SN+FT 8th-note hits→16th-note SN runs exist, plus 16th-note SN 5-runs and 16th-note double-bass (4-run) appear repeatedly. Slightly more pattern-heavy than same-level charts.
- **Lv5.35 主亡き機械人形のまなざし** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The Intro SN run section and pre-chorus BP→LP doubles appear. Without prior knowledge, combo is likely to break. Overall rhythm is hard to read due to odd time signatures. BPM is also higher so caution when attempting.
- **Lv5.40 黒髪乱れし修羅となりて** (DM/ADV)
    
    - **Lv5.50 卒業** (DM/ADV)
    - Both have many HH+BP→SN alternating runs. Cross style makes right hand + right foot easy to sync, but open stance makes hand-foot coordination difficult. Also hitting continuously throughout requires some volume tolerance.
- **Lv5.40 マネマネサイコトロピック** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The fast HH 8th-note section in the opening (BPM 210, HH→HH→SN→HH) and the BP→(rest beat)→SN→HH section in the chorus require some getting used to at appropriate level, but overall the rhythm is simple with no 16th-note runs. Accuracy is determined by how much stamina is preserved in the opening. Clear leans toward 逆詐称 as forced processing works, but connection is appropriate-level.
- **Lv5.45 four-leaf** (DM/ADV)
    
    - **Lv5.70 ほしふり** (DM/ADV)
    - Both have CY/HH 8th-note strumming and occasional 16th-note SN rolls — basic drum charts. "ほしふり" — watch the overtaking fill that easily breaks combo.
- **Lv5.45 LISTEN TO THE STEREO!!** (DM/ADV)
    
    - HH/CY quarter-note strumming, BP 8th-note doubles, and SN 16th-note runs are achievable for gauge maintenance, but near the end of the red chart an almost-identical CY 8th-note strumming pattern (with SN and BP) appears.
- **Lv5.50 Across the nightmare** (DM/ADV)
    
    - V-series ADV chart structure is unchanged, but some cymbals are now LC and FT is also used, making it slightly harder. Tom runs & SN runs and HH→SN alternating remain unchanged making it ideal for building skill, same as V series.
- **Lv5.50 Fractus** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Mainly 8th-note based, but many backbeats and tom runs, with LC involvement causing slight wide movements. Tests chart recognition — those who struggle with it may find accuracy hard to achieve.
- **Lv5.50 とびきりシュガータイム** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Unlike EXT, foot movement is consistent BP, but the hand portion is not greatly simplified. If you're not comfortable with strumming mixed with 16th-note offbeat rhythm, accuracy is hard to achieve. If high accuracy is stable on this chart, applying both-feet alternating can be directly used in EXT (Lv7.10).
- **Lv5.55 1/n** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Despite this level, the A-section has HH 16th-note strumming (HH4-SN-HH3). No feet involved, but without knowing 16-beat basics this can be a first-half killer.
- **Lv5.60 Lepton** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Individual variation appears greatly in the section where you process SN while stomping LP quarter notes mid-chart. Somewhat fast tempo plus 8th-note SN mixing makes it a fairly harsh chart for LP-averse players.
- **Lv5.65 800nm** (DM/ADV)
    
    - **Lv5.85 DOUBLE IMPACT** (DM/ADV)
    - Both have short 16th-note double-bass sections throughout.
- **Lv5.65 温故知新でいこっ！** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The chart is around mid-Lv4 level until the final chorus, but the HH triple section in the last chorus causes individual variation. Speed is somewhat fast so forcing through one-handed doesn't improve accuracy. Note: HH triple section in EXT becomes HH-SN alternating + both-feet alternating, with completely different chart tendencies between ADV and EXT.
- **Lv5.75 モラトリアム** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Almost no change from V series. Difficult sections concentrated in the first half (HH runs with SN/FT involvement and final 16th-note runs) and second half, but mid-chart is relatively easy making accuracy and gauge recovery achievable.
- **Lv5.85 BRE∀K DOWN! (GF&DM STYLE)** (DM/ADV)
    
    - **Lv5.90 此岸の戯事** (DM/ADV)
    - **Lv6.25 チカラ** (DM/ADV)
    - All have high-BPM 8th-note strumming for ADV. Perceived difficulty varies based on strumming tolerance. "チカラ" — 16th-note runs easily break combo, making accuracy lean toward 詐称.
- **Lv5.85 Predator's Crypto Pt.1** (DM/ADV)
    
    - **Lv5.85 Unbelief** (DM/ADV)
    - **Lv6.00 かつて描いた夢は、今も色褪せぬまま。** (DM/ADV)
    - **Lv6.20 涙のregret** (DM/ADV)
    - **Lv6.55 多極性ニューロンの崩壊による人間の末路** (DM/ADV)
    - All feature consecutive bass as a characteristic. Individual variation based on consecutive-bass skill. "Predator's Crypto Pt.1" has simple hand movement but slightly more backbeat. "Unbelief" has very simple hand movement leaning toward 逆詐称 — watch the 12th-note 6-run bass and isolated LP near the end for connection. "かつて描いた夢" has little hand involvement but BPM 150 8th-note is relatively fast with high note count, slightly 詐称-leaning. "涙のregret" has lower consecutive-bass proportion but many tom runs making it harder to connect.
- **Lv5.95 Let's Dance** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The Special phrase has quarter-note simultaneous stomping, but hand movement is low and once familiar it becomes a score-building section. Other sections also have many repeating patterns making connection and high accuracy easy once you grasp the patterns. However, each pattern requires different techniques, so individual variation based on strengths and weaknesses.
- **Lv5.95 triangulum** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Near the end a section consisting only of 3-tuplet double-bass exists. The only hand difficult section is the mid-chart 3-tuplet tom run, so accuracy varies greatly based on whether double-bass can be stomped.
- **Lv6.00 有頂天ビバーチェ (LONG)** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The chorus LC strumming is distinctive — an uncommon pattern at the same difficulty level causing individual variation. Many overtaking fills like SN→HT3→CY and SN→HT→FT2→CY, demanding a wide range of techniques for the difficulty.
- **Lv6.10 TIERRA BUENA** (DM/ADV)
    
    - No strumming — instead a chart hitting pads broadly centered on HT and LT. The HH×3→HT after the chorus ends determines individual variation based on rhythm-keeping ability. Players familiar with pad placement can clear even above level; connection is level-appropriate.
- **Lv6.20 お米の美味しい炊き方、そしてお米を食べることによるその効果。** (DM/ADV)
    
    - A chart with CY-SN alternating + 8th-note double-bass added appears 3 times. Similar patterns without LP involvement are common in late-Lv4 to early-Lv5, but both-feet alternating involvement makes this highly individual-difference prone. Some find it easy, but those struggling with strumming or alternating stomping may find it harder than the level suggests. Recommended alongside "X-Plan" ADV as a double-bass introduction.
- **Lv6.25 金輪際のエレジー** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Shuffle-rhythm HH backbeat appears in the chorus. At appropriate level the rhythm recognition easily trips up players, causing large individual variation in just that section.
- **Lv6.30 TENGU** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Density is level-appropriate but with considerable rhythm difficulty. Fine-detail HH 16th notes, backbeat bass, and 3-tuplet feints also appear. PERFECT rate is hard to raise making accuracy difficult.
- **Lv6.35 ECLIPSE 2** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Simplified from EXT to 8th-note strumming and HH/LC backbeat. Some sections have 16th-note offbeat HH strumming causing individual variation, but many repeating patterns mean adapting to overall patterns and 16th-note backbeat resolves issues.
- **Lv6.40 GET IT ALL** (DM/ADV)
    
    - HH 16th-note strumming appears in the opening (HH4-SN-HH3…), BP is in half-note intervals making it easy to focus on hands. Ideal practice for "RIGHT ON TIME" EXT and "夜に駆ける" MAS etc. Was adjusted upward from 6.00→6.40 this version.
- **Lv6.45 BOBBY SUE AND SKINNY JIM** (DM/ADV)
    
    - From the chorus, HH 2-beat + 8th-note consecutive bass, second-half high-speed tom runs, and the section processing HH+SN=left hand & CY=right hand alternately are the difficult sections. Without familiarity, those sections alone may cause dropout.
- **Lv6.60 Mother Tree** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Many patterns strumming CY in quarter notes while stomping LP in quarter backbeat — ideal practice alongside the above LP strumming songs. For accuracy, the LP+BP simultaneous stomping that appears mid-chart tends to be the difficult section.
- **Lv6.75 正論** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The chorus 正論 section has HHx2→SN, HH, SN (with bass) repeating — significantly different structure from V-series ADV. Players who struggle with this type of chart risk having gauge depleted and dropping out in that section, and even surviving it accuracy will be low. Some may feel it's Lv7-equivalent so treat it as a hidden difficult song.
- **Lv6.85 MODEL FT3** (DM/ADV)
    
    - A chart testing comprehensive hand skill including run-involved tom runs and LC+SN with HH alternating hits.
- **Lv6.85 PRIMAL SOUL** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Notes significantly thinned from EXT with less busyness than the level suggests, and ultra-fast tom runs have disappeared. However, the thinning is irregular making rhythm-keeping difficulty actually higher. May be hard to grasp first sight, but once familiar it could become a skill earner.
- **Lv6.85 The Least 100sec** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Chart structure close to V-era BSC. The mid-chart waterfall section that dominates upper difficulties has toms greatly simplified, potentially becoming a recovery section for some players.
- **Lv6.90 Reaching for the Stars** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The chorus has more notes than EXT, with uninterrupted CY 8th-note strumming. Also watch the HH→LP mimicking HH open-close.
- **Lv7.00 DAWN** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Despite being ADV, includes mid-speed and high-speed 8th-note alternating stomping and hand-mild mid-speed 16th-note double-bass — fundamentals of alternating stomping. Ideal for double-bass introduction, but the mid-chart 16th-note double-bass is individually variable making it a harsh difficult section for complete beginners.
- **Lv7.00 双つ雲と暁の奏** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The FT triple run from opening to mid-chart is quite tricky. It gets easier after that section, but accuracy is determined by the FT runs.
- **Lv7.00 林檎と蜂蜜** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Same as V series — a shuffle chart repeating CY→rest beat→SN in 8th-note triple rhythm. Unlike EXT, the right foot is simplified, so some may find rhythm harder to track. Was greatly adjusted from Lv6.60→7.00 this version.
- **Lv7.05 暗黒舞踏会** (DM/ADV)
    
    - **Lv7.15 Funky sonic World** (DM/ADV)
    - Both have continuous HH+BP→SN alternating runs or 2-beat strumming with note count considerably higher than same-level charts. "暗黒舞踏会" — BPM 175 various 2-beat patterns (LC+SN, HH+SD, CY+SN) and tom runs and 3-tuplets come without pause. Few rest sections demanding considerable stamina and concentration. However, no LP or consecutive bass so clear difficulty is appropriate. "Funky sonic World" — many high-speed strumming sections with high-speed fills in between making gauge loss easy. Late section has simultaneous HH+SN+BP hits at the same speed, potentially causing dropout at the very end for those who struggle.
- **Lv7.25 Brazilian Anthem** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Many unique patterns requiring familiarity including runs entering from backbeat, tom-linked strumming, and CY runs. Samba kick also appears mid-chart. If you can keep up with the tempo and tricky rhythm, it can become a skill earner.
- **Lv7.30 Little Prayer** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Some notes simplified from V ADV. Mid-chart HH-SN alternating is a cross-style-friendly pattern, slightly disadvantaging open-only players.
- **Lv7.30 SIX DIMENSION** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Mid-chart LP quarter-note strumming appears both on downbeats and backbeats. Especially the BP→LP alternating section like "Agnus Dei" MAS makes hand-foot sync hard, very dangerous for LP-averse players. Everything else is mild for the level — if LP is achievable it enters the skill-earner category.
- **Lv7.55 ツミナガラ...と彼女は謂ふ** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Many SN runs including some long ones (with bass), making timing recovery difficult. No LP but relatively much backbeat bass — accuracy hard to build without familiarity.
- **Lv7.90 DAY DREAM** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Same as V-series BSC — rhythm-difficult (17/16 time) strumming. Difficulty increases gradually toward the end with CY and BP increases. The final tom roll has gaps making GOOD and OK timing misses easy.
- **Lv8.10 百鬼夜行** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Double-bass, HH 16-beat, high-speed fills, and a final "一網打尽"-evoking last-killer — relentlessly testing comprehensive skill. Some players may achieve higher accuracy than the double-bass-specialized BSC.
