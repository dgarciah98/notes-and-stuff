# Selecting your profile
> [!tip] Guest profiles
> First time playing and don't have your own profile yet? Or simply don't want one?
> 
> You can play with the available guest profiles, ready for each play side. You'll have to set your own settings each time though!
> - 1P Profile: `nextage`
> - 2P Profile: `nextage2p`
> ![[../img/guest-profiles.png]]

Before getting into the game, make sure to pick your profile first. In Endless Dream you can change it in-game by using the Mod Menu, which you can open by pressing F5 on the keyboard, and it should look like this:

![[../img/raja-mod-menu.png]]

In this menu, click on `Show Misc Setting Window`, a window will pop up and at the end of the window there should be a `Player Profile` row with a dropdown and some buttons:

![[../img/raja-misc-settings.png]]

Click the dropdown, select your desired profile and click `Switch`, you're done! Take your time to set up your speed, SUDDEN and LIFT options, modifiers, etc.

![[../img/raja-player-profile.png]]

# Difficulty Tables
A more organized way to play any song and according to your difficulty preferences/ability would be making use of difficulty tables. 

There are ***many*** different kinds of tables, meant for any difficulty and skillset. You can find about the most known ones through this link (also taken as reference for this section): https://github.com/wcko87/beatoraja-english-guide/wiki/Difficulty-Tables

As a quick cheatsheet for anyone wanting the play, it would be as it follows:

Level signature:
- ☆ Level: IIDX-like level
	- From ☆1 up to ☆12
- ★ Level: Insane level, harder and beyond than IIDX's 12s
	- From ★1 up to ★25
- ★★ Level: Overjoy level
	- From ★★1 up to ★★8, first 5 levels correspond from ★21 to ★25
	- dont

Some tables may have different symbols or straight up different signatures (like Satellite/Stella), keep in mind just that "hollow" symbols correspond to IIDX-like levels and "filled" symbols are Insane charts.

### Tables breakdown

#### Beginners (up to IIDX 6th dan)
- [Stardust (ξ)](https://mqppppp.neocities.org/ChartView.html): From ξ1 to ξ13, covers up to some ☆7
	- ξ? corresponds to unrated charts
- [Starlight (sr)](https://djkuroakari.github.io/starlighttable.html): From sr0 to sr6, covers from ☆7 up to ☆9
	- sr? corresponds to unrated charts
	- sr! corresponds to gimmicky charts
- [NEW GENERATION 通常難易度表 (Normal table) (▽)](https://rattoto10.jounin.jp/table.html): From ▽1 to ▽9
- [通常難易度表 (Normal table)](https://darksabun.github.io/table/archive/normal1): From ☆1 to ☆9
#### Intermediate (between IIDX 6th dan and 9th dan)
- [Starlight (sr)](https://djkuroakari.github.io/starlighttable.html): From sr1 to sr13, covers from ☆7 up to ☆12
	- sr10 to sr12 equals to sl0
	- sr10 to sr12 covers ▽11 and some ▽11+ and ▼0-
	- sr13 equals to sl1, and covers some ▽11+, ▽12-, ▼0- and ▼0
	- sr? corresponds to unrated charts
	- sr! corresponds to gimmicky charts
- [NEW GENERATION 通常難易度表 (Normal table) (▽)](https://rattoto10.jounin.jp/table.html): From ▽9 to ▽11
	- ▽11 starts to get into sl0
- [通常難易度表 (Normal table)](https://darksabun.github.io/table/archive/normal1): From ☆9 to ☆12
#### Insane (IIDX 10th dan and beyond)
- [Satellite (sl)](https://stellabms.xyz/sl/table.html): From sl0 to sl12
	- sl0 covers ★1, sl12 covers ★18 and ★19
	- sl0 contains ▼0- and ▼0
- [NEW GENERATION 発狂難易度表 (Insane table) (▼)](https://rattoto10.jounin.jp/table_insane.html): From ▼0- up to ▼24
	- Except ▼0- and ▼0, level rating is equal to old Insane table
- [発狂BMS難易度表 (Insane table)](https://darksabun.github.io/table/archive/insane1): From ★1 up to ★25
- [Solar (so)](https://stellabms.xyz/so/table.html): Like Satellite but more gimmicky. From so0 up to so12
#### Overjoy
- [Stella (st)](https://stellabms.xyz/st/table.html): From st0 up to st12
	- st4 and st5 are divisions of ★★4
	- st6 up to st8 are divisions of ★★5
	- st9 and st10 are divisions of ★★6
	- st11 and st12 are divisions of ★★7
- [Supernova (sn)](https://stellabms.xyz/sn/table.html): Like Stella but more gimmicky. From sn0 up to sn11
- [NEW GENERATION Overjoy](https://rattoto10.jounin.jp/table_overjoy.html): From ★★1 up to ★★8, updated from old Overjoy table
- [Overjoy table](https://darksabun.club/table/archive/old-overjoy/): From ★★1 up to ★★7
- [joverjoy (仝)](https://www.firiex.com/tables/joverjoy): Alternative overjoy table, from 仝1 to 仝8
	- 仝99 is for unrated charts
	- 仝999 is for long note/gimmick charts
- [Dystopia (dy)](https://monibms.github.io/Dystopia/dystopia.html): Hard overjoy table, from dy0 up to dy10
	- dy0 to dy7 covers from st5 up to st12, dy8 and beyond is harder than st12

#### Specific skillsets
- Timing
	- [Timing Table (点)](https://plyfrm.github.io/table/timing/): Charts with Hard Judge, different ratings depending on difficulty and density
- Chords
	- [Ude table](https://lets-go-time-hell.github.io/Arm-Shougakkou-table/): Satellite level chord charts. From Ude0 up to Ude12
		- Ude0 corresponds to sl0 and so on
	- [16分乱打 (乱打)](https://lets-go-time-hell.github.io/code-stream-table/): Satellite and Stella level chordstream charts. From 乱打0 up to 乱打12 and from 重発狂0 up to 重発狂12
		- 乱打0 corresponds to sl0 and so on
		- 重発狂0 corresponds to st0 and so on
- Scratch
	- [Scramble table (SB)](https://egret9.github.io/Scramble/): Updated scratch table. From SB-1 up to SB12
	- [Scratch table (◎)](http://minddnim.web.fc2.com/sara/3rd_hard/bms_sara_3rd_hard.html): From ◎-1 up to ◎16
		- ◎7 equals to 灼熱 Pt.2 Long Train Running SPA in terms of difficulty
- Long notes
	- [Luminous (ln)](https://ladymade-star.github.io/luminous/table.html): Updated LN table. From ln1 up to ln26
	- [LN table ()](http://flowermaster.web.fc2.com/lrnanido/gla/LN.html): From ◆1 up to ◆26
- Delay
	- [Delay小学校 (///)](https://wrench616.github.io/Delay/): Satellite level delay charts. From ///-1 up to ///13	
	- [DELAY training table (早い)](https://kamikaze12345.github.io/github.io/delaytrainingtable/table.html): From 早い0 up to 早い32
		- 早い0 to 早い19 covers from ★1 up to ★19
		- 早い20 to 早い32 covers from st0 up to st12
	- [Delajoy table (dl)](https://lets-go-time-hell.github.io/Delay-joy-table): Hard delay table based on Stella ratings. From dl Beginner up to dl DELAYMASTER
		- dl0 equals to st0, and so on up to st12
