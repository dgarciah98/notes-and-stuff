# GITADORA — 逆詐称リスト · DrumMania (Lv6.50+)

> Songs whose actual difficulty is **easier** than their assigned level. DrumMania only. Sorted by level (ascending). All difficulty brackets included. Source: General GITADORA series wiki (all versions).

---

- **Lv6.50 Nature (GITADORA ver.)** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Regarding clears, the chart has an overwhelming number of BPM 230 quarter-note strokes making it lean toward 逆詐称, but there are a few BPM 230 triplet tom runs that appear several times. The shape is complex and you immediately return to CY/LC strumming, requiring fast arm movement. Connection difficulty is around Lv7.00.
- **Lv6.70 Saiph** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Outside the opening tom run and the interlude fill (SN→LT2→SN→FT2), it's mostly 2-beat with a little 8th-note BP run mixed in, making it easy to build accuracy if you have consistent stamina. Connection difficulty is around Lv6.55.
- **Lv6.75 Destiny lovers** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Fills and HH/CY runs are simplified from EXT, resulting in a chart centered on 8th-note strumming and 16th-note 3 and 5 runs. BP is a consistent quarter note making it easy to ride the tempo. If you get used to the chart tendencies, Lv6.55 players can aim for full combo.
- **Lv6.80 CALAMITY PHOENIX** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The 3-tuplet section in the first half is somewhat difficult, but everything else has straightforward strumming that's easy to PERFECT. Compared to "KAISER PHOENIX" ADV at the same difficulty, there are fewer cutpoints.
- **Lv7.00 Brown blizzard** (DM/ADV)
    
    - For this level, the hand density is low throughout and the occasional 16th-note tom runs are neither complex nor long. Only 5 LP notes, and the bass-including patterns are only rhythm-difficult overall. Accuracy is easy to obtain by this level's standards. Note the SN+CY 16th-note double run mid-chart.
- **Lv7.25 たまゆら** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The opening has 16th-note triple SN strumming, the middle has CY strumming, and the latter half has HT+cymbal combinations — chart tendencies are clearly divided by phrase. For this level bracket it's simple with few cutpoints and no LP at all. BP appears on the same rhythm as SN and cymbals making it easy to catch. Lv7.00 is the true requirement.
- **Lv7.40 MODEL FT2 Miracle Version** (DM/ADV)
    
    - The opening has 5-tuplet HT runs, but after that the density is low with mostly sparse strumming. The occasional tom engagement requires pad movement ability and chart recognition, but it doesn't measure up to old Lv7.40 songs. Around Lv7.15.
- **Lv7.60 Under The Shades Of The Divine Ray** (DM/ADV)
    
    - A chart like a strengthened "ランカーキラーガール" (EXT, Lv6.85), with BPM 192 alternating hits, 2-beat, and 16th-note triple fills as the main content. Around Lv7.40.
- **Lv7.65 Xenon** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Basic strumming and runs dominate, and the odd-time sections are also easy to read rhythmically. There's a double-bass section near the end but it's not long enough to significantly affect accuracy. Around Lv7.50.
- **Lv8.05 FIFTH GIG** (DM/ADV)
    
    - Popular for above-level attempts! As noted in the general/DM section, recovery after falling out of rhythm is difficult, but the BPM 120 low-speed patterns continue throughout making them easy to connect even by force. Individual variation appears in the CY double run section during the rest area and the final BP→LP repeating section, but there are no difficult sections for an Lv8 chart. The RV-era Lv7.75 is fair.
- **Lv7.15 CAPTAIN'S VOYAGE** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The opening CY triple section and the Verse LP quarter-note section are somewhat difficult, but otherwise there are many SN runs and simple strumming with no difficult sections. If the Verse SN+CY→HT→LT (16th note) can be connected, full combo is easy. Around Lv6.90.
- **Lv7.15 GETAWAY** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The first half has LP 8th-note stomping and HH 16th notes + irregular BP which is level-appropriate, but after that it becomes more thinned out than the V series. Around Lv6.90 class.
- **Lv7.15 Magic words?** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The 16th-note 2-run without a downbeat can slightly throw off rhythm, but being conscious of the both-feet simultaneous hits makes the hand patterns easier to recognize. Connection depends on the LT2-FT2-LT2 before the interlude and the late-song wide-movement section.
- **Lv7.20 ATOMS** (DM/EXT)
    
    - HH 16th-note triple runs and SN runs dominate. Feet are mostly consistent quarter notes making rhythm-keeping easy. Accuracy varies with stamina and run precision, but skilled players can aim for high accuracy from Lv6.80.
- **Lv7.20 LA ARENA ROJA** (DM/EXT) ※HOT
    
    - Recommended for Lv7 beginners! Compared to MAS, the samba kick and 16th-note CY are removed, simplified to quarter-note BP and shake strumming. Tom runs are also slow enough for one-hand processing. Overall Lv6.30 is fair, and even Lv6.60 players struggling with it can aim for full combo.
- **Lv7.20 White Stream** (DM/EXT) ※HOT
    
    - **Lv7.30 A - Astral Inner Flame ver. -** (DM/EXT)
    - All feature high BPM with HH-SN alternating as the majority. While playability varies by style, cross stance players can easily sync with the right foot, making accuracy achievable even above level. Around Lv6.80, Lv7.00, and Lv6.80 respectively. Note: "White Stream" was level-adjusted upward in this version as with MAS, but was over-adjusted into 逆詐称 territory.
- **Lv7.35 ROCKET MAN** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The LP quarter-note section and the FT quarter-note strumming + backbeat BP are somewhat tricky, but PERFECT rate is easy to maintain throughout the chart and speed is gentle enough to handle. Even accounting for the occasional fills, Lv7.10 is fair.
- **Lv7.40 イブの時代っ！** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The opening SN and tom runs are slightly tricky, but after that it's easy for Lv7.40 and accuracy is easy to build. The chorus has clear patterns and can even serve as a recovery section above level. LP only appears briefly in the opening and end, not problematic even if you struggle with it. Around Lv6.90.
- **Lv7.40 プロレタリア狂騒歌** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The late-chart SN roll sections are heavy and accuracy varies with run precision. However, the rest of the chart is quarter-note strumming dominant, and if you're careful about the small fills and even-pattern SN rolls, there are no particularly hard connection points. Around Lv7.15.
- **Lv7.45 Escape Velocity** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The song itself is rhythm-difficult, but there are no patterns more complex than short 8th-note fills (BPM 145 16th-note equivalent). Players who can consistently score S rank on historical pre-release event songs of this level with rhythm difficulty can also aim for high accuracy here. Around Lv7.20.
- **Lv7.55 Just Believe** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Popular for above-level attempts! The majority of the chart is simple quarter-note strumming and dance-beat. If comfortable with it, even above-level players can aim for skill score 120–135. High-speed HH/SN triple runs, LP quarter notes, and linear phrases appear but none are difficult for this level. Lv7.20 players can aim for full combo.
- **Lv7.70 Pray to the Earth** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Rhythm-difficult, but for late Lv7 the density is very low. No ultra-fast runs typical of AIKO OI songs, and other difficult sections are also mild. Around mid-Lv7 even by accuracy standards.
- **Lv7.80 EVIL OGRE** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Low LP count, and the hand difficulty is not hard for this level. The CY-SN alternating section has LP gaps, but keeping rhythm where there are no notes avoids throwing off rhythm. Around Lv7.50–7.60.
- **Lv7.85 Nature (GITADORA ver.)** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Some fills are complex in sequence, but 4-beat strumming + BP quarter-note stomping makes accuracy easy to obtain. A short skip double-bass (LP with bounced rhythm double-bass) and CY double run appear in the second half, so clear difficulty depends on those. If you absolutely cannot stomp them, dropping LP is an option. Even with this approach, if you connect everything outside the mid-section, S rank is achievable depending on front-half phrase capture and PERFECT rate.
- **Lv7.95 ペルセポリス** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Many unique patterns not found in other songs, such as pad runs with various timbres assigned and alternating tom runs (sounds like tabla). Despite unconventional patterns and rhythm-difficult elements, the density is sparse for this difficulty with no extreme cutpoints or sections requiring left-foot technique. Chart places emphasis on hand technique. Around Lv7.80.
- **Lv8.10 ジュピターガンズノベル** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Overall irregular rhythm is common, but note count is low and density/patterns are not at Lv8.10 difficulty. No fast connection-difficult fills either, so both clear and connection difficulty are around late-Lv7.
- **Lv8.15 キルト** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The strumming pattern is simple for this difficulty with no complex LP combinations or difficult fills. Compared to "FLOWER" EXT which has similar tendencies, the density is lower and the only tricky part is 3-tom fills. Around Lv7.95.
- **Lv8.20 Atalante** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Popular for above-level attempts! A tom-run specialist chart typical of Kozo songs, but even compared to "Desert Rose" etc., there are no extremely difficult sections. Overtaking fills and 6-tuplet tom runs are frequent, but BPM is slow enough for forced processing. Around Lv8.10–8.15 equivalent.
- **Lv8.30 Aion** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Strumming-dominant chart with occasional tom runs and double-bass, but all are quite simple for Lv8, and BPM is slow at 164. The ultra-fast runs in MAS are all removed, making high accuracy easy to achieve. Around Lv8.00–8.10.
- **Lv8.30 戦場のタクトシュトック** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Despite odd time signatures, the patterns are simple making PERFECT and combo easy. Around late-Lv7 class. Connection depends on whether you can handle the rhythm-difficult simultaneous stomps + SN roll at the very end.
- **Lv6.50 Far Beyond Blackened** (DM/MAS) ※HOT
    
    - Consistently slow 16th-note shake and 8th-note backbeat bass. As long as this type of chart isn't a weakness, Lv6.25 players can easily aim for full combo.
- **Lv6.70 Lead Me** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Outside the 16th-note 2-run BP, extremely simple strumming and HH backbeat make up the chart. Watch the one LT+CY near the end and Lv6.50 players can aim for full combo. The backbeat section uses 8th-note 2-run BP rather than simple quarter BP, but continuously hitting HH in 8th notes makes syncing hands and feet easy without confusion.
- **Lv6.80 pandora -追憶の彼方へ-** (DM/MAS)
    
    - BPM 170 8th-note strumming continues for a long time, but it's more relaxed than high-speed strumming songs at the same difficulty. The reverse tom (LT2→SN)→tom run in the Special can be a cutpoint, but no other notable difficult sections exist. Around Lv6.60.
- **Lv7.00 ナイト・オブ・ロンド** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Mild HH strumming is the main content with low density and only 652 total notes. Watch the 24th-note HT 4-run in the B-section and the 2-run bass that appears twice in the chorus and connection is easy. Around Lv6.70.
- **Lv7.00 夜に駆ける** (DM/MAS)
    
    - HH3→SN-HH2 repeating and RIGHT ON TIME-type 16-beat continues throughout, but there is not a single LP and BP is almost entirely consistent quarter notes making it easy to focus on hands. Effectively the same level as RIGHT ON TIME (Lv6.70). Even after the major adjustment from Lv8.50→7.00 this version, still usable as a skill earner. For connection, watch the small amount of toms mid-chart — not particularly complex and BPM is slow enough for forced processing.
- **Lv7.05 DREAM RUSH** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Compared to EXT, sections requiring sequence construction like LT-FT-SN4 wide movement and HT-LT-CY overtaking have increased. However, everything else is mainly simple strumming, making accuracy relatively easy to achieve. Around Lv6.90.
- **Lv7.05 スキスキ星人** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Consistently dance-beat and basic strumming throughout. Fills are SN-run based with little complex movement. Around Lv6.70.
- **Lv7.10 Dreams on the road (LONG)** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Most patterns are the same tendency as "Dreams in the night" (Lv6.65), and the main difficult sections are only the tom combination in the early part of the second Phrase A and the late-chart 16th-note triple run. BPM is slow enough that both patterns can be processed one-handed. Around Lv6.70.
- **Lv7.15 LAST SCENE** (DM/MAS)
    
    - The unconventional HH and 4-beat triple bass are characteristic, but BPM is not particularly fast and chart density is low. Even if the 2 high-speed (BPM 205 16th-note equivalent) overtaking fills are difficult sections, they don't qualify as Lv7 difficulty. Around Lv6.75.
- **Lv7.20 Fly Above -Band ver.-** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Compared to EXT, 16th-note runs have been added in the opening and pre-chorus. The opening tom run (LC+SN→SN→LT2→LC+SN) includes simultaneous hits requiring double-stroke. Everything else is mainly simple strumming, and with attention to the few mandatory one-hand sections (SN+HH→SN→HH, SN+CY→SN→CY, tom run→SN+FT), it's not particularly hard. Around Lv6.95.
- **Lv7.20 ××警告** (DM/MAS)
    
    - If you can handle the 4-beat strumming with ghost SN (16th-note off-beat), even above-level players can aim for high skill. If you can also do the SN-HT-LT fills, even higher accuracy is within reach. Around Lv6.95.
- **Lv7.25 Forever free** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Aside from the chorus becoming 8th-note strumming and some fills being distributed to FT, it's not significantly different from the V-era chart. The chorus backbeat bass density is not high, and even if the fill rhythm is slightly hard to read, calm handling at this non-fast BPM makes full combo achievable. Given V-era difficulty was Lv63, Lv6.90 is fair.
- **Lv7.25 モラトリアムノオト** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Consistently dance-beat and basic strumming throughout. Fills are SN-run based with little complex movement. "モラトリアムノオト" has higher note density than "スキスキ星人" and longer SN runs can become combo-killers, but the rest of the chart is comparable. Around Lv6.75.
- **Lv7.30 mint candy ☆ citrus drop** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Mild tempo HH strumming with various toms in an open-stance recommended chart. Bass is mostly 4-on-the-floor and LP combinations are only simultaneous stomping near the end. The mild BPM also makes fills easy to connect. Overall there are no Lv7 elements at all and note count is under 700 making density low. Individual variation appears in the forced 16th-note HH triple (HH2→HH+SN), but even so around Lv6.80.
- **Lv7.35 はなまるぴっぴはよいこだけ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - The FT strumming shape is distinctive and there are LP quarter-note + HH backbeat catch points, but compared to songs at nearby levels there are no extreme cutpoints or technically difficult sections, making accuracy easy to achieve.
- **Lv7.40 She's the bug (LONG)** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Bounced shake and backbeat bass mixed simple strumming is the main content. No difficult fills or runs at all, and players with late-Lv6 technique can connect without difficulty. Even accounting for precision difficulty and LONG-song adjustment, around Lv7.05–7.15.
- **Lv7.50 Dust In The World** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Consistently 3/4 (6/8) time 8th-note strumming chart. Some stamina and strumming tolerance needed given the long strumming sections, plus 3-beat familiarity is important, but no 16th notes appear at all and BP is consistent, making full combo achievable for Lv7.10 players with good stamina management. Also ideal for cross-style-only players to establish open-stance basics.
- **Lv7.50 Ugly Duckling** (DM/MAS)
    
    - For a Hizuka chart, there are few open-stance recommended sections and it's playable even in cross style. A few sections require one-hand SN doubles, but Lv7.50 is still an overestimate. Around Lv7.25.
- **Lv7.55 素直な気持ちで** (DM/MAS)
    
    - BPM 168 8th-note strumming is the main content, LP combinations are few, and no complex fills at all. Some 16th-note run endings have simultaneous hits but not to connection-difficult levels. Around Lv7.15.
- **Lv7.55 ブランコ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - No particularly difficult points overall. The consistent LP is only a ghost-note SN like "Homesick Pt.2&3" EXT, with no difficult tom runs or foot combinations. Even accounting for the slightly long song, Lv7.15 is fair. Open stance recommended near the end where you strum while hitting toms.
- **Lv7.70 SMILE FOR YOU** (DM/MAS)
    
    - The song is only 1 minute 25 seconds, so note count is very low at 666 (about 940 notes on a 2-minute basis), making chart density very low for Lv7.70. Some characteristic patterns like SN-backbeat tom wide movements and SN+tom triple×4 appear, but BPM 190 12th notes (= 16th-note BPM 142.5) are slow and can't be called locally difficult. No problematic LP patterns at all. Around Lv7.60.
- **Lv7.85 全弦開放 (イーマイナーイレブン)** (DM/MAS)
    
    - BPM 190 2-beat strumming and short double-bass are the main content, but phrases are short with many rest sections so stamina isn't heavily demanded. Almost no troublesome high-speed fills, and combo maintenance is easy. The closing fill-to-simultaneous + bass pattern at the end is slightly tricky but no Lv7.85-appropriate difficult sections are visible. Around Lv7.70 by accuracy standards. Connection difficulty is appropriate.
- **Lv7.90 5-10** (DM/MAS)
    
    - The occasional 16th-note 2-run BP and good-cool-style tom runs are characteristic, but BPM is very low at 118 making forced processing easy. LP combination sections are few, and high accuracy is easy for Lv7.90. Around Lv7.55. Note: near the end the strumming changes to cowbell HT — V-series veterans should be cautious on first sight.
- **Lv7.90 Twinkle Wonderland -Sparkle Wonderland-** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Consistently BPM 188 strumming chart. Slip-beat and many backbeat SNs make the 427 technique hard to use, but if high-speed strumming is a strength it feels easier than "GLIDE" (Lv7.65) of similar tendency. For connection, watch the sudden CY 16th-note runs.
- **Lv7.90 傷だらけの硝子** (DM/MAS)
    
    - LP quarter-note + CY strumming and HH shake are the main content, and the required technique is not high for this level. Some hard-to-read fills appear from the chorus onward, but once understood they're manageable. Around Lv7.50.
- **Lv8.00 Break Down** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Monotonous strumming and short fills are the main content. Some 8th-note BP sections appear but are short, and there are absolutely no cutpoint-worthy difficult sections for Lv8. Around Lv7.70.
- **Lv8.00 ラキ☆ハピDAYS!** (DM/MAS)
    
    - BPM 200 2-beat and 16th-note run sections exist, but otherwise there are no difficult sections making overall chart density mild. Compared to "恋する☆宇宙戦争!!(GITADOROCK ver.)" of similar tendency, the near-absence of tom runs makes around Lv7.85 fair.
- **Lv8.15 Fly far bounce** (DM/MAS)
    
    - A comprehensive chart with alternating stomping, 16th-note HH, dkdk and various elements. Individual variation especially in the mid-chart alternating stomp + unconventional ride section, and late-chart double-bass + CY-tom simultaneous combinations mean first-sight is not relaxed. However, no extreme combo-killers or high-speed run fills — if you can reliably catch combo and PERFECT in the 8th-note strumming and 16th-note HH sections, high accuracy is easy. Around Lv8.00. For connection, watch the single forced-processing tom and flam, and don't let the final alternating run (no bar line) throw off your rhythm.
- **Lv8.15 漆黒のスペシャルプリンセスサンデー** (DM/MAS)
    
    - BPM 200 double-bass and HH 16th-note triple run sections are tricky, but both are short, and everything else is mostly simple strumming or rest sections. Late-Lv7 skill is enough to handle it, and since difficult sections are only in the first half, accuracy is also easy to achieve. A chart similar in tendency to "ホーンテッド★メイドランチ" EXT (Lv8.65) with the double-tom sections removed and double-bass shortened.
- **Lv8.20 ヒコーキ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Same as V series — a chart where you hit toms in a backbeat bass rhythm. HH almost never appears creating a distinctive feel with individual variation. The complex-sequence fills are frequent but all in 8th-note patterns so calm handling keeps things stable. Note count is 1000+ making it surprisingly continuous so watch stamina management, but once familiar with the chart it's mild for Lv8.20. Given V-era difficulty (Lv70), around Lv7.85.
- **Lv8.25 リドル** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Simple strumming and tom runs are the main content, and the only Lv8 elements are a 9-run double-bass and the pre-late-chart fill (tom run→both-hands simultaneous). BPM 180 speed aside, high PERFECT rate is also easy, making Lv8.25 an overestimate. Around Lv7.95.
- **Lv8.30 50th Memorial Songs -Flagship medley-** (DM/MAS)
    
    - The majority is 8th-note strumming dominant with low chart density, making it easy to achieve high accuracy for the level. The Winning Eleven and early/late sections especially have almost no obstacles to phrase combo completion. The Power Pro and MGS sections are mainly even-count 16th-note HH, but double-stroke and reflex processing are sufficiently possible. Overall around Lv7.90, even qualifying as individual-difference 逆詐称.
- **Lv8.30 And The Dead Ravened For** (DM/MAS)
    
    - CY strumming + LP quarter-note strumming is frequent, but mild HH strumming and shake-beat patterns continue. Even if the pre-late-chart tom fill→SN+FT simultaneous is a connection cutpoint, it's easy by Lv8.30 standards. Around Lv7.90.
- **Lv8.30 BAD HERO** (DM/MAS)
    
    - LP-included FT strumming, 8th-note BP-triple-included HH strumming, HH backbeat and tom runs — comprehensive chart tendency, but strumming is quarter-note dominant with no 16th-note or higher runs or complex double-bass. No Lv8 elements by accuracy standards. For connection, watch the tom run→both-hands simultaneous.
- **Lv8.30 イノセントバイブル** (DM/MAS)
    
    - BPM 210 8th-note equivalent (= BPM 105 16th notes) means strumming and fills are somewhat fast, but each individually is in the mild range for this difficulty. Individual variation from LP strumming and 2-run BP, but even so around Lv8.00.
- **Lv8.30 NO MORE SORROW** (DM/MAS)
    
    - From mid-chart, LC strumming, short double-bass, LP quarter-note strumming and 8th-note 3-run BP left-side-heavy patterns increase. However density is mild for this difficulty, and careful attention to double-bass and LP strumming transitions makes high accuracy easy. Outside the HT-LT-FT staircase fills there are no cutpoints, and Lv8.00 is the true requirement.
- **Lv8.40 DUAL STRIKER** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Simple strumming and dance-beat dominate making PERFECT rate easy to build. No cutpoints outside the late-chart overtaking fill. Around Lv8.15.
- **Lv8.45 Six String Proof** (DM/MAS)
    
    - A chart simplified from "Nebulas" with 2-run bass mixed backbeat sections and 24th-note-mixed fills. The opening has HH 16th notes and close-motion reproduction LP+BP simultaneous — unusual patterns not seen in other songs — worth noting. Overall note density is also thin. While comprehensive skill is tested, it's in the weaker range for Lv8.45. Around Lv8.30.
- **Lv8.55 Just Believe** (DM/MAS)
    
    - High-speed HH runs appear from the very start so some preparation is needed, but otherwise it's mostly dance-beat (quarter BP-LP simultaneous + backbeat HH) and LP quarter note + CY strumming in simple patterns. Despite LP being disproportionately high in the graph, most are simultaneous with BP, and the required technique is mild for this difficulty level. Around Lv8.25.
- **Lv8.55 ミラクル・スイート・スイーツ・マジック！！** (DM/MAS)
    
    - BPM is over 200 and high-speed, but LP quarter notes and gap-filled HH strumming are the main content making required technique quite mild for Lv8.55. Dense fills exist but they don't rise to the level of sequence-difficulty or connection-difficulty, and high accuracy is easy to aim for. Around Lv8.20.
- **Lv8.60 Our Faith (Faithful MTL Remix)** (DM/MAS)
    
    - A comprehensive chart testing quarter/8th-note strumming, mid-to-high-speed fills, dance-beat, and 16th-note double-bass, but the individual required techniques are not particularly high. Even if the longer mid-chart 16th-note dkdk section is a cutpoint, connecting everything else makes high accuracy easy to achieve. Around Lv8.35.
- **Lv8.85 MEGALOVANIA** (DM/MAS)
    
    - High-speed runs and linear phrases (BPM 240 16th-note equivalent) are tricky, but no complex sequences appropriate to the difficulty level are required. LP technique-wise, BPM 120 low-speed double-bass is sufficient, and as long as you watch the RC 16th notes, recognition is easy. Around Lv8.55 by accuracy standards. Note: one FT double-run fill (SN→LT→FT2→CY) exists.
- **Lv8.95 天弓ノ舞** (DM/MAS)
    
    - A wide-layout chart overall with 16th-note sections, consistent LP, double-bass and other elements testing comprehensive skill. However, there are few unreasonable patterns or forced-processing sections, making it a straightforward chart for late-Lv8. Players with solid skill can aim for high accuracy even below the appropriate level. Lv8.80 is fair.
- **Lv9.00 THE LAST OF FIREFACE** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Popular for above-level attempts! BPM 224 8th notes are the main content with generally low density, and the difficult sections requiring sequence study are also patterned making it very approachable. Rhythm-difficult elements exist but have little impact on accuracy, and even above-level players can expect high skill scores. Around Lv8.30.
- **Lv9.00 ギタドラシカ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Short 16th-note double-bass, irregular LP, and tom runs — a comprehensive chart. While comprehensive skill is demanded, the chart is straightforward for this level and pure technique is tested. The opening CY-tom 16th-note wide movement is likely to break combo, but overall accuracy is easy to build and there are no standout difficult sections, making it inappropriate for Lv9. Around Lv8.80.
- **Lv9.00 ランカーキラーガール** (DM/MAS)
    
    - 2-run and 4-run double-bass appear frequently, but overall density is low for Lv9, and there are no long double-bass sections, complex fills, or LP combinations at all. The only cutpoint is the overtaking tom run in the first half, and adapting to the BPM makes high accuracy achievable. Around Lv8.80. Popular for above-level attempts! The chart's simplicity makes it accessible to red-name players with sufficient stamina, and an A rating (skill 131) offers good skill value.
- **Lv9.20 RISE UP** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Popular for above-level attempts! Outside the long fill section at the end of the Intro, the BPM 168 double-bass in Phrase B, and the fills in Phrases C and D, it's simple dance-beat repeating, and late-Lv7 players can enter the target range by clearing those sections. Even considering connection difficulty, Lv8.95 is fair.
- **Lv9.20 ディストピア・ロックヒーロー (LONG)** (DM/MAS)
    
    - A high-speed double-bass section (BPM 209) appears near the end and there are many high-speed fills throughout, but the basic pattern is quarter-note strumming with many mild sections, and connecting where connectable makes PERFECT rate and combo rate easy to build. Lv9.00 is the true requirement.
- **Lv9.25 Invisible Farewell** (DM/MAS)
    
    - The main Yuri (TAKA) part is weak for Lv9 — watch the variable-speed HH (8th⇔16th switching) and there are no other difficult sections. The Ash (PON) part has persistent flams making it relatively tricky, but this also doesn't have Lv9 elements. Only the Smile (Akhuta) part has appropriate rhythm and chart complexity for the level. Note: BPM changes 3 times along with part changes so first-sight caution needed. Also, RC strumming sections always come with LP strumming — approach cautiously if that's a weakness. Despite individual difference factors, overall level is low making Lv9.00 fair.
- **Lv9.25 プログレなぞなぞクイズのテーマ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Despite the hard-to-grasp rhythm-difficult feel, the only main difficult section is a double-bass section with unconventional hand movement. That section does cause individual variation, but the double-bass rhythm itself is slow at BPM 136 equivalent for this difficulty. There are sections to watch like LP-section time signature changes and hard-to-read HH 16th notes, but each is weak relative to the difficulty, making Lv8.95 fair overall.
- **Lv9.30 Kazekiri Rumble** (DM/MAS)
    
    - BPM 174 8th-note FT sections with HT/LT combinations, and CY 8th-note strumming sections with 16th-note dkdk combinations dominate, but connection is easy if you can clear other songs at this level. Double-bass appears in 2 phrases but without length, and even dkdk-averse players can aim for high accuracy. Around Lv9.15. Note: almost no rest sections and continuously hitting throughout — adequate stamina is required.
- **Lv9.35 Arena Argenticuria (LONG)** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Variable-rhythm strumming and 16th-note double-bass are frequent, and being LONG the total note count is 2518 — the highest in GITADORA series history. However, the long performance time keeps density low and there are almost no patterns that force combo breaks making phrase combo easy to build. Compared to the same artist's "Arena Deiporta" MAS-D (Lv8.80), perceived difficulty is the same, and even accounting for LONG-song adjustment, Lv9.00 is fair.
- **Lv9.35 The ULTIMATES -reminiscence-** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Performance time is very long and note count is high at 1807, but the original songs were significantly thinned out in medley form resulting in almost no forced-processing or unreasonable patterns. Yvya-song characteristic 24th-note high-speed fills are frequent, but no odd-count fills exist making them processable. As long as you watch the tempo changes, accuracy can be obtained without particular problems. Around Lv9.05.
- **Lv9.40 1716 SAHARA MOON** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Like "1816 WIND", SN+CY 8th-note runs and recognition-difficult strumming are the main content, but variable-speed SN runs and odd-time LP 8th-note strumming are added. However, BPM is slower than "1816 WIND" and complex ghost notes are almost absent, making accuracy clearly easier. Connection is difficult due to the same 17/16 extreme rhythm-difficulty as "DAY DREAM" and persistent variable-speed SN runs, but around Lv9.10 is sufficient for accuracy.
- **Lv9.40 ヘンテコ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - LP quarter-note strumming and overtaking-involved tom combinations are tricky, but overall patterns far below Lv9.40 continue throughout. The long high-speed double-bass section near the end causes individual variation, but even accounting for that, around Lv9.00.
- **Lv9.80 MODEL FT4** (DM/MAS)
    
    - A comprehensive chart with complex bass 正論 patterns, 16th-note dkdk, and fountain fills. A section requiring fountain fills while processing ultra-high-speed double-bass (24th notes = BPM 232.5 16th-note equivalent) exists making connection and even clear difficult for above-level players. However, the only truly late-Lv9 difficult section is this fill section, which is infrequent and phrase combo is also easy to build. Overall around Lv9.60.
