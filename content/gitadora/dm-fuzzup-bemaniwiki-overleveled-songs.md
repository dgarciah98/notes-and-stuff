# GITADORA FUZZ-UP — 逆詐称リスト · DrumMania (Lv6.50+)

> Songs whose actual difficulty is **easier** than their assigned level. DrumMania only. Sorted by level (ascending). All difficulty brackets included.

---

- **Lv6.50 PLAY** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Always 4-beat strumming + basic backbeat bass throughout, very straightforward. The last fill is easy if read as 8th-note triplets HT-LT-BP. Lv5.60 players can even aim for full combo.
- **Lv6.60 テレキミ** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The 16th-note off-beat driven runs from the upper charts are gone, and the odd 16th notes are also simplified to SN-HT2. Most of it is basic backbeat, and even considering connection difficulty it's around Lv6.35.
- **Lv6.60 マッハマンのテーマ ~ GITADORA Victory Mach Mix ~** (DM/EXT)
    
    - The base pattern is HH offbeat with SN 16th-note runs mixed in. If you can handle the opening fill and the final tom run, Lv6.40 players can aim for full combo.
- **Lv6.90 TRUTH behind U** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Heavy on off-beat rhythm difficulty from time signatures, but outside of the second Phrase A, it's mostly simple strumming with little movement in the 5-hit runs. Overall density is also low. Around Lv6.50.
- **Lv7.00 如雨露姫が世界征服** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Up until the final high-density tom run, the main content is 2-beat strumming with a few offbeats mixed in. Even the final phrase isn't too hard with prior study. Around Lv6.60.
- **Lv7.00 グッバイ宣言** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Basic strumming and dance-beat patterns throughout, simpler than the rating suggests. Around Lv6.65. For connection: watch the backbeat LP and late-chorus SN3-HT-SN3-LT (16th off-beat start).
- **Lv7.20 LA ARENA ROJA** (DM/EXT)
    
    - →旧曲
- **Lv7.20 White Stream** (DM/EXT)
    
    - →旧曲
- **Lv7.20 テレキャスタービーボーイ** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Basic strumming and dance-beat patterns throughout, simpler than the rating suggests. Around Lv6.90. For connection: watch the pre-Phrase A overtaking fill (SN-HT-LT2-CY).
- **Lv7.30 I LOVE SUNFLOWER** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Dominated by BPM 190 2-beat strumming and HH+BP→SN alternating hits. No other difficult sections and enough rest sections for good stamina management. Around Lv6.70. Note that HH+BP→SN alternating is disadvantageous for open stance players.
- **Lv7.40 I LOVE uUu ～Holy Graduation～** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Dominated by BPM 180 2-beat strumming and HH+BP→SN alternating hits, with added 2-beat strumming and 16th-note SN triple rushes. Still equivalent to old Lv7.10-range strumming songs. Note that HH+BP→SN alternating is disadvantageous for open stance players.
- **Lv7.40 Garden feat. yosumi** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Outside the fills, it's a very relaxed 8th-note chart for this difficulty. Many wide patterns, so open stance helps. The late 8th-note double-bass section is the main challenge, but the hands only do simple LC quarter-note strumming, letting you focus on the pedals. Around Lv6.80.
- **Lv7.45 泥の分際で私だけの大切を奪おうだなんて** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Very high BPM of 210+ but quarter-note strumming with very honest patterns. Some individual variation from occasional backbeat bass and LP, but still nowhere near the rating. Around Lv7.20. Watch the 16th-note tom run + double bass combo.
- **Lv7.50 琥珀のくちづけ ～まり花＆一舞 duet edition～** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Recommended for Lv7 beginners! After the opening fills, only extremely simple strumming remains. Even considering stamina, Lv6.75 is the true requirement.
- **Lv7.50 Black Apple** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Well-rounded chart, but 8th-note rhythm tom combinations (HH-SN-tom) are the main content. Players with Lv6.90 level skill can aim for high accuracy.
- **Lv7.50 passing shower** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Constant 8th-note tom patterns + 16th-note backbeat bass repeating, with some LT synced to the backbeat bass. Even accounting for the risk of developing bad habits from the repeating patterns and the pedal pattern change in the final phrase, it's around Lv6.80.
- **Lv7.50 スイーツはとまらない♪** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Constant orthodox 8th-note patterns (SN-HH3-SN…) throughout. BPM 204 8th notes are easy enough to handle even with one-hand processing, making it easy to aim for S rank (≈skill 120 pts) even above your level. If you can connect the 16th-note tom runs mid-chart, you can push even higher. Around Lv6.85.
- **Lv7.60 テレキミ** (DM/EXT)
    
    - Only basic strumming for this difficulty, with low overall density. Once you understand the early HH run and the left-hand-start (16th off-beat) fills, Lv7.20 players can aim for full combo.
- **Lv7.70 PLAY** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Very high BPM of 220+ but quarter-note strumming with very honest patterns. Around Lv7.20. Watch the final 16th-note linear phrase.
- **Lv8.40 Harmonia** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Unusually for a PON song, no forced-connection difficulty patterns. The Verse A HH-SN mixed section is hard to read, but everything else is processable with full singles — 16-beat, dance-beat etc. If you can keep up with the volume and speed, even above-level players can connect. Around Lv7.95.
- **Lv8.50 I LOVE COSMOS** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Consistent BPM 197 2-beat strumming throughout with no other notable patterns. If the mid-chart tom runs stabilize, above-level players can expect high skill scores. Unlike "I LOVE SUNFLOWER" and "I LOVE uUu", the 16th-note double BP runs are split to BP→LP, reducing strain on the right foot. Around Lv8.20.
- **Lv8.70 レジスタンス** (DM/MAS)
    
    - Requires well-rounded skill including consistent LP-centered foot technique, unconventional fills, and 24th-note mixed HH strumming, but overall it's straightforward with no extreme cutpoints. Around Lv8.50. Many elements applicable to other HOT songs in this version — recommended for building overall skill.
- **Lv9.40 GLACIER EXPRESS** (DM/MAS)
    
    - The long RC strumming sections with mixed 16th-note triplets are the main content. LP appears often but mostly as both-feet simultaneous hits, so chart flow is easy to follow. Drum solo patterns are restrained and 24th-note fills are infrequent. While the triplet sections do affect accuracy, the Lv9.40 rating is an overestimate — around Lv9.00.
