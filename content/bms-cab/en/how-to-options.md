> 1. Set up modifiers (Random, Mirror, Gauge, etc.) with 1P Start or Q on keyboard
> 2. Set speed options, GAS and Lift or Sudden with both 1P+2P starts or, more comfortably, Q+W on keyboard
> 3. Configure your skin settings by pressing keyboard's F12 on music select screen
> 	a. Alternatively, configure your skin by going to Skin Live Editing in Mod Menu (F5 in keyboard > Skin Live Editing)
